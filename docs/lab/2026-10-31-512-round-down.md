# 5.12 gen-5 step rule: lab record (2026-10-31)

All runs on loopback (`127.0.0.1`) in a cloud container, with servers started
for the run. No public server, no real accounts.

## Setup

- Server: this fork, branch `seihai-512-round-down` (gs2lib from
  seihai-gs2lib `seihai-512-round-down`), built with GCC 14 on Ubuntu 24.04.
  "Before" is branch `seihai` at `083306a` (gs2lib `496f6dd`).
- Lab config, not in the repo: `serveroptions.txt` with `127.0.0.1` and UPnP
  off, and `G3D14097` added to the `newmain` line of `allowedversions.txt`.
- Lab listserver v2 (`listserver.py` from the handoff) on 14900.
- A Login lobby: a small Python script that takes the gen-5 game login and
  answers with one uncompressed `PLO_SERVERWARP` (178)
  `0,Lab Server,127.0.0.1,14802`, as the real Login server does.
- Client: Seihai `seihai-mae --version 5.12`, headless under Xvfb, pointed at
  the lobby. Captured with `tcpdump -i lo` on the gserver port and decoded
  with a gen-5 decoder that steps either way.

## Results

| Run | Seihai 5.12 | C>S frames (server's own rule) | S>C frames |
|---|---|---|---|
| After (round-down for 5.12) | lobby login, follows the 178, logs in to Lab Server, level from the board, asks for `wbowi1.png` and gets it | 5 of 5 decode exactly | 10 of 10 decode |
| Before (stock round-up) | the same on screen | frames 2 to 5 fail plain zlib; see below | 10 of 10 decode |

5.12 sends every frame zlib-compressed. Its second frame (`PLI` 46) is
11 bytes: 5.12 steps twice, stock gs2lib three times. The third step only
touches the last 3 bytes, which are inside zlib's Adler-32 trailer. Stock
gs2lib then fails the checksum, falls back to raw deflate (the workaround
for the 2.x clients' "bad Adler-32"), and on that fallback rewinds the
iterator one step. Because the two rules differ by exactly one step on
every body under 16 bytes, that rewind puts the server back in step, and
the before run worked by accident.

With the rule, the server decodes every 5.12 frame with plain zlib, never
takes the fallback, and encodes its own zlib and bz2 frames to 5.12 the way
5.12 decodes them. In these runs every S>C compressed frame was 16 bytes or
longer, where both rules step 4 times; a shorter one would only decode on the
client with the rule.

Gserver logs: no `[zlib]` errors in either run.

## Unit test

seihai-gs2lib `tests/Gen5StepsTest.cpp` (`-DGS2LIB_TESTS=ON`): passes with
the rule; with the rule turned off it fails 5 checks.
